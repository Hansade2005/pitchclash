What's in the game

52 teams20 national sides and 32 clubs, each with its own kit, squad and rating.
Champions cupsGroup stages home and away, two-legged knockouts with aggregates, and a one-off final.
Nation cups & leaguesWorld and Africa nation cups, plus four home-and-away club leagues with tables.
Real match rulesOffside, cards and red cards, penalties, injuries, 5 subs with fitness, extra time and shoot-outs.
Online with a friendHost a room on your Wi-Fi and play a friend on another PC, or 2 players on one PC.
Match-day atmosphereTwo commentators, chants, kick-off jingle, walk-out music, goal celebrations and slow-motion replays.
Edit modeRename teams, squads and cups, change kits, import your own badges, export your edit file.
