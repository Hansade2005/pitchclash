## Generated flags (99 nations)

All other flags are original artwork generated by `tools/flags/generate_flags.mjs` from simple
geometric specs that follow each flag's published layout. They are not downloaded files.

Flags with coats of arms, text or detailed emblems use a **simplified stand-in** shape instead of
the official emblem, so they are approximations: Albania, Algeria, Angola, Australia, Bahrain,
Benin, Canada (star instead of maple leaf), Cape Verde, Croatia (no checkerboard), Cuba,
DR Congo, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Egypt, El Salvador, Equatorial Guinea, Fiji, Georgia,
Guatemala, Haiti, Honduras, Hong Kong, India, Iran, Iraq, Kenya, Kuwait, Lebanon, Malaysia,
Mauritania, Mozambique, Namibia, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Papua New Guinea, Paraguay,
Portugal, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Africa, South Korea, Syria,
Tajikistan, Tanzania, Tunisia, Turkey, Uganda, Uruguay, Uzbekistan, Venezuela, Wales,
Zambia, Zimbabwe. Replace these with official artwork if exact designs matter.
