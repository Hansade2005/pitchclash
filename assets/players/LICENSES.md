# Player models: sources and licences

Files: `footballer_a.glb`, `footballer_b.glb`, `footballer_c.glb`, `footballer_d.glb`, `keeper.glb`

Generated by the scripts in `tools/players/` (see `tools/players/build_all.sh`). Every third-party input
allows free commercial redistribution inside a game.

## Body, face shapes, skeleton, skin weights
- **MakeHuman base mesh, modelling targets, `game_engine` rig and its weights**, as bundled in the MPFB2 add-on
  (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2, `src/mpfb/data`).
  Licence: **CC0 1.0** (see `LICENSE.ASSETS.md` in that repository).
- The MPFB2 add-on **code** (GPL-3.0) was only used as a tool, inside Blender, to generate the meshes. No MPFB code is
  part of these files or the game. MakeHuman states that its output models are not covered by the GPL.

## Skins, eyes, eyebrows, hair, shoes
- **MakeHuman system assets pack** (`makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip`,
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html).
  Used: skins `young_caucasian_male`, `young_caucasian_male2`, `young_african_male`, `young_asian_male`,
  `middleage_caucasian_male`; eyes `low-poly` + brown/brownlight/blue textures; eyebrows `eyebrow001/002/003/005`;
  hair `short01`, `short02`, `short04`; shoes `shoes06` (used as boots, recoloured).
  Licence: **CC0 1.0**. The asset files say: "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020".
  Textures were downscaled and recoloured, and the geometry was decimated.

## Kit
- The shirt, shorts and socks are material zones cut into the MakeHuman body mesh and offset from it by the build script.
  They are original work for this project, so no third-party licence applies.

## Animation
- **CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu), BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com),
  taken from the mirror https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap.
  Licence statement from CMU: "This data is free for use in research and commercial projects worldwide."
  Requested acknowledgement: *"The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was
  created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."*
  Takes used: 137_28 (Idle), 16_36 (Jog, Dribble), 127_07 (Run), 143_01 (Sprint), 10_05 (Pass), 10_02 (Shoot),
  13_39 (Header), 79_69 (Celebrate), 90_16 + 140_01 (Fall).
  The motion was retargeted, made in-place, looped and retimed, and some clips have added keyframes.
- Hand-keyed clips (Tackle, ThrowIn, KeeperIdle, KeeperDiveLeft/Right, KeeperCatch, KeeperThrow) are original work
  for this project.
